# 生成提示词

生成方式为内置 imagegen。原图作为配色和氛围参考。

## 澄汐图标

Use case: logo-brand. Create a brand-new static Android adaptive launcher icon concept for 澄汐, a local-first AI assistant. Input image: the user's illustration is a mood/color reference only. Take only the moonlit sea, deep ink-blue and luminous cyan tones. Independent original geometric symbol: one chunky ivory crescent rising above two asymmetrical, smoothly tapered tidal ripples, on a solid deep navy square field. A restrained light mint/cyan on the water, crisp vector-style edges, flat color blocks, instantly legible at 24 px and easy to reconstruct as editable paths. Optical centered, main mark fits within the central 60% to survive Android circle and squircle masking. Artwork bleeds to the square edges, do not draw rounded outer corners, no phone mockup or tile shadows. No eyes, character, fish or whale silhouettes, bubble, text, numerals, glitter, tiny detail, watermark, or imitation of any existing logo. Deliver a single square finished icon, no design board or surrounding grid.

## 小汐设定稿

Use case: stylized-concept. Create the primary front three-quarter full-body character concept for 小汐, an original miniature moonlit-sea sprite, designed as a mobile desktop mascot and future animation reference. Input image: mood and color reference only, taking dark cobalt water, luminous cyan accents and the moonlight. Design a NEW and distinct little non-human water spirit with a rounded teardrop-shaped floating body, two small webbed mitten-like flippers, no legs and no forked mermaid tail, a dark cobalt hood-like head with a single blunt turquoise curl, two clear big oval navy eyes with tiny highlights, and a small simple off-white crescent clasp on a short cyan scarf. Positive calm neutral expression, compact top-to-bottom silhouette, tiny ribbon mirroring a ripple, subtle cool gradients only. The character should read at 24–48 px: bold color shapes, clear separation of eyes and scarf, restrained detail, friendly and confident, neat almost cel-shaded mobile game mascot rendering. Full body neutral idle pose, slight three-quarter turn, all silhouette edges visible, center composition with modest padding. Genuinely transparent alpha, no scene, props, floor, ground shadow, text, labels, additional characters, sheet divider, sparkle particles, watermarks, copied outfit, lace or human portrait. One isolated full-body model, no logo or brand icon.
